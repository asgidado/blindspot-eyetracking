## Data (never committed)

- **ChestX-Det** (Hugging Face `MedOtter/ChestX-Det`): radiologist instance annotations on NIH ChestX-ray14 frontal
  radiographs. Cite Lian et al., arXiv:2006.10550 and arXiv:2104.10326. Images originate from the **NIH Clinical
  Center** ChestX-ray14 release and require attribution to the NIH Clinical Center. Fetched only by `make films` after
  the human accepts the dataset terms; stored under gitignored `data/films/`.
- Synthetic phantoms (`mock/phantoms/`) are generated by this repo and carry no third-party licence.
